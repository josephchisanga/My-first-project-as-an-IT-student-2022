Ndola Central Constituency Mapalo Ward - Depot Zone

Ndola Central is a constituency of the National Assembly of Zambia. It covers central parts of Ndola and a rural area to the east of the city in Ndola District of Copperbelt Province

Mapalo Ward is one out of the many wards in Ndola Central Constituency located on the central part of Ndola on the Copperbelt due north of the Kafubu River which is one of the water sources in Ndola. The area is one of the fast growing townships in Ndola, having a population of approximately 41,837 with no piped water and sewerage services.

Mapalo Ward is a condusive area to live in,its community friendly ,on the central part of town which makes it easy to maneuver around the city. It has enough schools catering for all family setting a police post and others organisations such as ZESCO, KABUFU WATER AND SUWARAGE COMPANY, ANIMAL VET just to mentiona few. The people here live in harmony despite coming from diffrent works of life and having different social standings.

The community house many facilities, places and has good sites for spending time with loved ones.
